# Student_Performance_Analysis
##Analysis of Factors Affecting Student Performance and Resit Predictions
(rough draft)
In this study, I will be analysing various factors that could affect pupil peformance.


Loading, EDA, cleaning, manipulating, answering questions, visualising and perform basic predictive ML through training and testing data
Questions:
Who are the top 5 highest and lowest peforming pupils
What are the common relationships and patterns for the above pupils
What is the relationship between access to resources and exam success
How does access to resources alongside internet access and tutoring affect scores?
Do private school pupils outpeform public pupils?
Link between motivation, parental education and exam outcome
SEN influence on exam score (any provisions in place?)
School distance from home,study hours and motivation
Gender comparisons on performance
Predictions in 6 months. If each pupil were to be given the same access to resources, internet and tutoring, will there be similar outcomes? How much % improvement for underperfoming pupils?
